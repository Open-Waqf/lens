# Privacy Policy for Sahifah Lens (العدسة)

**Last Updated: February 2026**

## 1. Our Commitment

Sahifah Lens is built on the principle of **Data Sovereignty**. We believe you should own your data. Our privacy policy
is simple: **We do not collect, see, or store your data.**

## 2. Data Processing & Storage

* **Local Processing:** All document scanning, image filtering, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR) are performed
  entirely on your device. No image or text data is ever sent to our servers or any third-party cloud.
* **On-Device Storage:** Your documents are stored in the **Origin Private File System (OPFS)** and **IndexedDB** on
  your local device. This data is private to the application and is not shared with other websites or apps.
* **Offline-First:** Sahifah Lens does not require an internet connection to function.

## 3. Personal Information

* **No Accounts:** You do not need an email, phone number, or identity to use Sahifah Lens.
* **No Tracking:** We do not use analytics, trackers, or cookies to monitor your behavior.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Sahifah Lens uses the following local libraries:

* **Tesseract.js:** For local OCR. No data leaves the device.
* **Capacitor:** To interface with your device's native camera and file system.

## 5. Security of Data

While Sahifah Lens stores your documents in a private directory, the files are stored **unencrypted** locally. We
recommend:

1. Using device-level encryption (e.g., FileVault, BitLocker, or Android Encryption).
2. Using our **Encrypted Backup** feature to create password-protected copies of your library.

## 6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your data, you have total control over it. You can delete all your data at any time using the *
*Nuclear Reset** feature in the app settings.
